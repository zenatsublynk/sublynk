How to add it in Gmail
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) and choose See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new (or pick an existing one).
  4. Click into the signature box and paste with Cmd+V (Mac) or Ctrl+V (Windows).
  5. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
How to add it in Outlook
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook, go to Settings, then Mail, then Compose and reply.
  3. Under Email signature, click into the box and paste with Cmd+V or Ctrl+V.
  4. Click Save.
